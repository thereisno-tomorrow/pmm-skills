# Attribution and distribution boundaries

The instructions, workflow and illustrative examples in this repository are the
author's own writing. The MIT license in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers that material only,
not another author's source text, courses or trademarks.

**Positioning:** April Dunford's framework supplies the causal sequence from
competitive alternatives through relative capabilities, value, best-fit customers
and category. The broader stage/review/activation structure is the author's
implementation. See [positioning lineage](skills/dunford-positioning/references/lineage.md).
No book, official workbook, course recording or original corpus is included.

**Narrative:** Andy Raskin's strategic narrative, set out in his essay ["The Greatest Sales Deck I've Ever Seen"](https://medium.com/the-mission/the-greatest-sales-deck-ive-ever-seen-4f4ef3391ba0),
opens a story with a big change in the buyer's world rather than with the product.
Marcus Andrews built Narrative Design on that work and credits Raskin and Brian
Halligan as the people who taught him most about it. Andrews' public [2020 article](https://marcusandrews.substack.com/p/why-narrative-design-will-replace)
and [2022 interview](https://www.launchnotes.com/blog/narrative-design-with-pendos-director-of-product-marketing-marcus-andrews)
explain the broader Narrative Design lineage. The interview connects audience
change and consequences to an adaptation that can be used before buying tools;
it also discusses difficulty and the product's supporting role. This package's
five working labels, review units, evidence handling and continuity are an
independently written implementation, not a reproduction of an official course.
See [narrative lineage](skills/narrative-design/references/lineage.md).

**Research:** practitioner-developed investigation and correction procedures inform
the authored method. Worked records and their source corpora are not included.

**What is not included:** no course text, private guide or original corpus is
included. Nothing here licenses them, and no permission to reproduce them is
asserted. While developing the method the author also consulted private practitioner
guides and worked examples; none of that material is included.

The authors and publishers of these frameworks do not endorse this repository.
The examples are illustrative and describe no real company or customer.
