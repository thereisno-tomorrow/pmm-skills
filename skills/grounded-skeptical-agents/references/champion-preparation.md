# Champion preparation

Use this mode to prepare an internal advocate for a specific organizational decision. A pitch can supply the champion's case; the task is preparing that internal decision, not automatically rewriting the pitch or deciding whether to switch.

Establish what approval or participation the champion seeks, what they may commit, and the evidence behind the case. Where supported, distinguish who benefits, pays, implements and authorizes. A title does not establish a priority or veto. When stakeholder evidence is missing, provide tentative questions and a bounded brief rather than invented objections.

For each consequential concern, distinguish a misunderstanding from a legitimate mismatch in requirements, incentives, product capability or readiness. Give the champion a truthful response supported by available proof. Name what remains unanswered and who must supply evidence or make a decision. A clearer explanation cannot create a capability, budget, authorization or implementation capacity.

Check the commitments embedded in the proposed case: savings, dates, staff effort, displaced systems and promised outcomes. Do they follow from current evidence, and can the champion make them? Keep incompatible requirements visible. Do not turn one stakeholder's interest into another's approval, or invent consensus to complete the story.

Recognize conditions already satisfied; do not manufacture resistance or require adoption-level proof for an appropriately bounded investigation. Match the next ask to readiness: requirements clarification, documentation review, a bounded pilot and purchase request carry different commitments. Narrow or decline a case that cannot honestly meet the decision conditions.

Return a compact readiness judgment, prioritized stakeholder concerns and evidence-backed response material, unresolved proof gaps or disagreements, and the smallest justified next ask. Suggested responses must preserve real limitations. No scripted committee, fixed number of roles or persuasion at any cost is required.
