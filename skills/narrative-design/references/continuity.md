# Continuity and upstream handoffs

## Keep a small current record

Use the host's existing suitable workspace and evidence ledger. If a new run needs
files, create a small narrative folder with an index and only the current stage
document; add later stages as needed. In a method library, ask for the company/output
location before creating a company run. Never overwrite another exercise.

The index records the narrative job, governing positioning/product/audience records,
evidence locations, stage order/status, actual review decisions, current branches,
next action and affected dependencies. The active document contains the argument
and review packet, not a transcript. Reuse the host decision log where appropriate;
do not require a new database or duplicate evidence into a second ledger.

To resume, read the index, current packet and approved inputs it uses. Recover only
the material context. If approval cannot be identified, state the uncertainty and
request the actual decision; polished prose and an assistant's assertion are not
evidence of human approval. A narrow repair reuses valid approved stages.

After a real decision, update the packet and index. Mark historical review requests
as completed/superseded and keep next actions current. An approved internal draft
does not become external-ready copy or company-adopted strategy.

## Positioning handoff

Use the current positioning assembly, its evidence and challenge disposition when
available. Equivalent inputs suffice without Dunford. Retain alternative branches,
fit conditions, product responsibilities, proof gaps and unresolved validation.
Confidence and logical status are different: an inference is not an observation
simply because someone has high confidence in it.

In a Dunford run, Stage 8's trend/point of view can inform Change; it may also have
been legitimately omitted. Stage 9 supplies current positioning and Stage 10 its
challenge disposition. Neither guarantees a defensible external-change narrative.

Dunford Stage 11A develops a buyer conversation and may already contain an adequate
narrative. Reuse it rather than reconstructing it automatically. If this method is
selected to develop or revise that artifact, record the relationship in the existing
artifact plan and maintain one identifiable current artifact/review status. A
narrative review covers its stated unit, not other Dunford stages or artifacts.
A changed positioning review contract must be explicit; this skill cannot silently
waive it. Standalone narrative use does not require running Dunford first.

## Research and strategic conflicts

For a missing fact, first check existing sources. If it can change the narrative
decision, name the exact question, strongest rival, affected stage, needed evidence,
authorized routes and effort/stop limit. Use a focused companion research pass if
available; otherwise use the host's suitable tools. Return findings, changed claims,
unknowns and evidence handles to the pending narrative stage. Update owning records
and relevant research handoff content; do not rerun unaffected work.

At an access/budget stop, retain the gap and offer bounded hypotheses, narrower
claims or a pause. More public-source collection cannot stand in for unavailable
customer selection reasons or observed outcomes. No outreach/private-data upload
authority is supplied by a research request.

If audience, alternative, purchase object or differentiated value conflicts with
governing positioning, identify the disputed premise and consequence, then seek a
positioning decision. A narrative working choice can be provisional if explicitly
selected; do not represent it as adopted company strategy.

## Propagate revisions

When evidence, scope or a user decision changes, identify the earliest affected
premise and the stages/claims/translations that depend on it. Reopen that stage;
mark dependent approvals/artifacts stale and retain independently supported work.
Review the revised stage before rebuilding dependent units. Separate branches
when different audiences or premises require incompatible arguments.

A product-scope correction may require narrowing Solution alone, or may expose an
earlier unsupported Adaptation/Impact premise. Explain which applies. Do not delete
real customer difficulties merely because the offer cannot solve them, and do not
expand the offer to preserve the story. Keep the current assembly identifiable.
