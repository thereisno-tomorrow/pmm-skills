# Check the analyses and hand them off

## Source fidelity

Recheck interpretations that drive conclusions against the original record and exchange. Verify speaker, dates, source versus paraphrase, estimates, conditions and numerical comparisons. Inspect complete text where a shortened view could alter meaning. A retrieved post establishes a public statement, not its truth. In supplied-corpus mode cite provided IDs and do not imply external verification.

## Read the analyses against one another

Compare the map, individual founder briefs and relevant existing analyses. Look for misleading grouping, incompatible claims, missing links and useful tensions. Before declaring a contradiction, check the same population, question, period and conditions. The founder reading a proposal may be a different audience from the buyer receiving company content.

Read limits/gaps alongside headlines. Shared limits can suggest consequential questions, but several weak public signals do not prove a business bottleneck. Preserve genuine tensions; do not assume every disagreement must be reconciled or all files merged.

## Implication audit

For material strategic implications, make the dependency visible:

| What we know | What we assume | What follows if true | What changes if wrong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Apply this where it affects the handoff. Public silence does not establish buyer interest or valuable whitespace. Founder conviction does not establish product performance. Engagement does not establish commercial behavior. Carry conditions into downstream documents rather than laundering assumptions into diagnoses.

Revisit incomplete sources when missing material could change a consequential conclusion. A full interview may supply positions absent from the X sample. Respect the collection boundary: explain any additional check needed rather than silently expanding it.

## Correct and deliver

Correct the authoritative interpretation, note the material change and source/reason, and update dependent implications. Keep original evidence and superseded interpretations recoverable. Link related records rather than maintaining competing copies of conclusions.

Hand off requested components:

- Category map: questions, positions, reasoning, evidence and uncertainty.
- Founder briefs: substantive thinking, contributions, evolution/tensions and sources; comparison where relevant.
- Evidence index/reading guide: routes to complete records and exchanges.
- Implications/open questions: conditional interpretations, dependencies and useful next checks.
- Coverage/currency: window, search limits, missing viewpoints, unresolved context and claims requiring refresh.

Explain what subsequent work can use and what remains undecided. A short entry page routes to authoritative records rather than duplicating everything. Do not call the map market consensus or the handoff adopted company strategy. Sending, publishing and further strategy/writing are not implied by preparing a handoff.
