# Collection and claim ledger

Use these fields as optional working notes, not a requirement to fill every cell or code every statement. Preserve existing user formats. Examples below are illustrations, not evidence. Narrative maps and substantive founder briefs remain the outputs; tables support traceability.

## Scope and retrieval

Record category/question, named people and identity basis, window/timezone, languages/geography, permitted source types, collection budget and stopping condition.

| Lane | Exact query/source | Date range | Retrieved at | Raw hits | Unique IDs added | Limits/gaps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Record failures and inaccessible sources as gaps. Do not add overlapping raw hit counts and label the sum unique. Report what was processed separately from everything surfaced. A source date is different from the date it was retrieved. Engagement is a dated discovery metric, not a weight in an opinion poll.

## Item and context trail

| ID / canonical URL | Author / role | Published | Origin / duplicate-of | Parent / quoted / surrounding IDs | Context state | Disposition and reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Context states can be sufficient, partial, unavailable or not needed. Preserve which source actually contains the words: a founder reply is not its parent's text. Excluded and unresolved items remain accounted for; an unresolved item may contribute a clear standalone clause without supporting its opaque reply.

Reconcile: processed unique items = kept + excluded + unresolved. Report duplicates separately and link them to the retained canonical item. An item may support multiple claims but still counts once in this disposition inventory. If processing is partial, give the unprocessed count when known.

## Claim coding

| Claim ID | Exact proposition and conditions | Source/context IDs | Speaker / date | Scoped stance | Attribution basis | Evidence limits / conflicting IDs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use a short faithful paraphrase or necessary brief excerpt. Preserve estimates as estimates and opinions as opinions. Mark analyst interpretation explicitly. Do not attach a numerical confidence score without a justified calibration method.

## Map and founder view

For each argument, state:

- The proposition and why the supplied positions actually disagree (or why they do not).
- Each evidenced position, speaker, date/conditions and source/context IDs.
- Shared ground, missing opposition or unresolved evidence.
- A distinguishing observation, or a value trade-off that evidence alone cannot settle.

For each founder, use claim rows only where helpful beneath a substantive brief covering reasoning, distinctive contributions, evolution and implications. Avoid global “pro” or “anti” labels across independent issues. A missing row means not observed within scope, not absence of belief. For updates retain a short old interpretation → new interpretation → evidence/reason trail. Read [analysis guidance](analysis.md) and [review/handoff](review-handoff.md) for the full outputs.

## Synthetic attribution boundaries

**One proposition.** P1: “Open specifications cannot fund maintenance.” F1 replies: “Wrong.” With P1 as complete parent, F1 rejects that categorical funding claim. It does not establish a particular revenue model or support every open project.

**Independent axes.** F2 says “Publish specifications openly” and F3 says “Validate every release.” Those support openness and quality control together. Do not turn them into opposing camps.

**Several propositions.** P2: “Logs should be mandatory, stored forever, and vendor-funded.” F4 replies: “The second part is wrong.” F4 rejects indefinite retention. The exchange does not establish a stance on the other two clauses.

**Missing context.** F5 says “Exactly” to an unavailable parent. Keep the item unresolved. Do not use it to endorse the most plausible topic in nearby search results.

**Sample silence.** No collected post discusses pricing. Report “pricing position not observed in the collected window”; propose a pricing-specific search if useful. Do not conclude that the founder avoids pricing or that buyers want a pricing-led campaign.
