# Completion, diagnosis and handoff

## Completion gate for each domain

Record a compact status after the pass, with evidence/reason against each applicable check. “Not applicable” needs a reason. These gates assess substantive work, not heading presence or document length.

| Check | Evidence needed to pass |
| --- | --- |
| Domain question and boundary | Central questions answered on that domain's terms; parked downstream implications do not replace the investigation |
| Evidence fidelity | Consequential factual claims trace to read evidence or are explicitly unverified; attribution, class, dates/version and conditions retained |
| Explanatory result | Mechanism, prerequisite, failure boundary, comparison or purchase implication is explained; consequential verdict and reason are usable downstream |
| Operational depth | Specifications or equivalent delivery/organizational detail sought where relevant; feature-only coverage caused a targeted deeper attempt or an explicit blocked/unavailable status |
| Fair comparison | Compatible metrics, units, denominators, dates/versions, configurations and purchase scope, with arithmetic exposed |
| Counter-reading | A serious alternative/contrary source tested, or exactly what could not be tested is declared; resolved evidence need not receive a manufactured objection |
| Gap loop | Ends with actionable unknowns, affected decisions and routes; shallow first-pass gaps received follow-up where possible within scope/budget |
| Current readable record | One current verdict; superseded claims/dependents repaired; facts, attributed wording, calculations and interpretation distinguishable |

**Complete for the stated research scope:** all applicable research checks pass; remaining internal/customer-validation unknowns are explicit and do not masquerade as findings. **Bounded:** named open gates limit use, but supported findings can still be delivered. **Incomplete:** central mechanism/question or required coverage remains unaddressed. A budget/access stop is not a passed gate. State the smallest next check and effect on handoff. Completion of outside-in research never implies customer validation.

## Cross-domain diagnosis

After included dependent passes, write a short opening verdict and an explanation of company/product role, capability boundary, buying conditions and system, alternatives, surviving strengths/barriers, current marketing purchase support and decisive unknowns. Link evidence-owning entries rather than duplicate facts. Separate the mechanism-based explanation from observed commercial incidence. A “no supported distinction on examined dimensions” diagnosis is legitimate.

Read the [confidence calibration](examples.md#confidence-audit-and-handoff-repair). For each consequential proposition audit:

`Exact proposition | evidence and dependence | existence/performance/prevalence/causality | confidence and reason | strongest credible rival | disconfirming observation | smallest discriminating check | safe wording`

Grade the proposition, not its surrounding paragraph. Documented capability may have stronger support than achieved performance, and much stronger support than commercial causality. Use high/moderate/low/not established with reasons rather than citation-count scores. Compare each serious rival's predicted observation with what is actually available. Design checks that could favor the rival; wins alone bias a commercial explanation. Include lost/stalled/no-decision or failed-deployment records where authorized, and inspect internal-data definitions/missingness before treating them as precise.

Rewrite any overstatement in its originating analysis and dependent diagnosis/parked note/handoff. Keep supported surviving claims; do not remove useful hypotheses merely because they remain hypotheses. Report known, hypothesized and decision-critical unknown separately.

## Positioning-research handoff

Provide the following inputs with their evidence and applicable limits:

| Input | Carry forward |
| --- | --- |
| Product scope | Current capabilities, prerequisites, exclusions, tier/version and responsibility boundaries |
| Alternatives | Relevant approaches/suppliers; actual deal alternatives only when evidenced |
| Differences | Bounded capability/delivery differences under compatible conditions; parity or unknown where appropriate |
| Candidate value | Mechanism linking difference to buyer consequence; achieved outcomes graded separately |
| Candidate customer fit | Problem/prerequisite conditions versus observed customer pattern; no automatic beachhead selection |
| Buyer language | Attributed customer vocabulary; vendor terminology labeled separately |
| Proof and unsafe claims | Available support, dependence and validation gaps; wording that would overstate it |
| Decision risks | Unknowns that could alter alternatives, value, fit, category or diagnosis |
| Validation backlog | Prioritized questions, required evidence/route, and owner only when known |

The next positioning stage should be able to use the conclusions without repeating their derivation, while still having traceable sources available. This is not authority to lock positioning. Link deliverables, state each pass's status, what the research can support, blockers and what would change the interpretation.

In the author's original case study, the confidence audit occurred later than some positioning work. Placing the audit and explicit handoff here is a deliberate reuse improvement, not a claim about the original run's exact chronology.
