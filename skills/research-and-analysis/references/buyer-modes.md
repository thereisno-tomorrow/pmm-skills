# Buyer research within company pre-positioning analysis

Use this procedure only when public discussion research is needed for the named company's buyer pass. The output feeds candidate buying conditions, language, objections and validation gaps in the positioning-research handoff. It does not select the target segment or validate demand. A complete company study need not run a social-post collection if other suitable evidence answers its buyer questions.

## Public demand signals

Before collection record question, window/version slices, surfaces, discovery frames, exact initial queries, inclusion/exclusion rules, selection order, unit, author/repost controls, coding scheme and effort/coverage ceiling. Derive limits from the task; no universal quota or theme threshold applies. Preserve explicit coverage requirements. Log amendments and reasons so promising results cannot silently steer collection.

Discover by jobs, pain, alternatives, switching, attempted use and countercases such as abandonment/no need. If a segment is explicitly the subject, target it and state the selection frame; otherwise do not preselect the desired segment through query terms.

Verify included material at its original accessible source or clearly identified faithful capture. Search/model output is a lead. Retain canonical post/comment IDs, author, dates, route and relevant text. Classify question/opinion/hearsay/use report/company announcement. Code job/prerequisites, stated role, trigger, alternatives, objection, claimed outcome, payment and version only when stated; otherwise unknown. A date slice does not establish version used.

Check relevant affiliation/incentive signals within authorized public access. Distinguish confirmed, possible, none found after stated checks, and unchecked/unknown. Company/staff statements remain company voice. Report affiliated material separately rather than discarding inconvenient evidence. Avoid unnecessary personal profiling.

Preserve opposing replies, corrections and edits. Record hidden branches, unavailable comments and rejected/unverifiable units. Reconcile separately:

- Result occurrences versus canonical posts/threads.
- Opened/read threads versus captured comments, distinguishing top-level from nested answers.
- Coded units versus distinct authors; repeated posts do not establish independent support.
- Planned, attempted, blocked, skipped and unattempted work; actual terminal boundaries.

Displayed comment totals are not denominators of captured top-level answers. Crossposts do not become independent evidence. Discovery may be complete while reading, affiliation checks or coding remain incomplete. Visible exhaustion describes an inspected interface, not the entire platform index.

Report themes, vocabulary, opposing evidence and counts with actual denominators, dates, routes and selection limits. Convenience samples do not estimate market size, general prevalence, best-fit customer, willingness to pay or purchase causality. Partial findings remain explicitly partial. Stop at the declared ceiling or report incomplete coverage and continue accessible in-scope work when some routes fail.

Keep user, champion, evaluator, economic buyer and approver roles distinct in the company analysis. Vague “finance teams” language does not establish a controller's budget authority. Cross-vendor alternatives can inform this company's buyer/alternative passes, but standalone cohort inversion or buyer-desk dossiers are outside this skill.
