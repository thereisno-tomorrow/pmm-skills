# Evidence and gap protocol

For each consequential observation retain a stable ID, exact claim, source/title/location, original claimant, publication/version and retrieval date when known, scope/conditions, source class, grain, and what it establishes / does not establish. Mark unavailable metadata unknown. Separate quotation, observation, reported outcome and interpretation. Cite evidence IDs or source locations from analysis; avoid duplicating records across domain registers.

Use informative claim headings, a short opening verdict/reader summary in analyses, and tables for genuine comparisons. Mark factual observations, attributed quotes, your calculations and **Read:** interpretations distinctly. Follow destination formatting rather than requiring Obsidian syntax. For supplied/private sources use authorized document IDs and provenance; do not invent URLs or treat them as public verification. Training knowledge can guide questions but does not silently supply company facts. Keep shared firmographics separate; aggregators are discovery leads until original support is inspected.

| Axis | Distinctions and use |
| --- | --- |
| Source class | Vendor-controlled; third-party structured/reporting; independent of the subject. Inspect original attribution and method: filings/journalism can repeat vendor assertions; an unaffiliated author is not automatically a reliable tester. |
| Grain | Positioning/value claim; feature/named capability; specification/explicit number, condition, limit or result. Seek operational detail where relevant. Numeric specificity establishes neither truth nor independence. For services investigate delivery conditions, capacity and quality controls rather than imposing software or hardware architecture. |

Record shape: `E1 | claim/conditions | source/location | original claimant | dates/version | class + grain | establishes | does not establish | interpretation`. Use readable prose if a wide table would obscure reasoning.

Deduplicate lineage: a release and syndicated copies are one originating claim. Distinguish several observations from several independent tests. Resolve conflicts by identity, scope, version, date and method; if unresolved, retain both and narrow the conclusion. Show arithmetic inputs and units. A benchmark needs workload and comparable conditions/method before supporting a performance claim.

Record exact queries, date, intended question, surface, meaningful exclusions and actual stop boundary. Proposed queries are not executed queries. Existing evidence may already answer the question; avoid redundant collection.

Before a full study's broad sweep, write question/source/contrary-query rows for company identity, product, buying conditions, alternatives and commercial activity. Map primary documentation, support/releases, pricing, case/customer material, partners, filings/procurement where relevant, transparent measurements and independent accounts. The sweep creates a navigable evidence base and routes deeper questions; it is not completed analysis. Reuse an existing adequate map rather than repeat it mechanically.

Inspect terminology and statistical definitions, not just the search phrase. Publication, modification, retrieval and product-release dates differ. A snippet is a lead; mark any snippet-only retained assertion unverified. Roadmap promises and repaired historical defects do not establish current shipped behavior on every configuration. Keep durable source locations/captures under workspace rules without copying private or third-party corpora into a reusable package.

## Route the gap

Ask who would record the fact, and why. Before calling a gap publicly unanswerable, inspect plausible surfaces or explain why that cannot be done within this task.

If a broad query fails, change the **recording surface** and domain vocabulary: commentary cannot measure frequency; the vendor's careers page does not describe the buyer's approval duties; funding databases miss government suppliers. Log the route hypothesis and the relevant read result, including when the source supplies only a proxy. A source appropriate for one quantity may not answer the decision's actual question.

| Gap | Plausible next surface |
| --- | --- |
| Capability/compatibility/limits | Versioned docs, release/support notes, datasheets or suitable tests |
| Outcomes/failures | Operator accounts, independent tests, authorized customer evidence; vendor cases retain selection limits |
| Adoption/base rate | Administrative data, government statistics, transparent surveys with matching definitions/denominators |
| Buying roles/approval | Procurement artifacts, role descriptions, authorized interviews; a role description alone does not establish purchase behavior |
| Ownership/IP | Original filings, patent claims/status and ownership records; a patent alone is not commercial defensibility |
| Deal alternatives/economics | Authorized CRM, win/loss and interviews; public landscapes do not establish deal frequency |

Track gap ID, originating pass, decision affected, evidence, routes attempted, next route, discriminating observation and status. Route to **search better**, **ask an authorized source**, or **not established in the searched record**, stating coverage. Track access blockers separately: unread, paywall, missing permission, empty response, deleted material or failed retrieval. A legitimate alternative surface may help; this method grants no authority to cross access boundaries.

Confidence reflects source fit, independence, method and surviving alternatives rather than citation count. Explain high/moderate/low/not-established judgments; a fixed score cannot conceal a decisive unknown. End each pass with actionable gaps or explain why no material open gap remains within scope.

The live register carries the current claim, not competing correction entries. Preserve change history through the destination's recoverable history or separate development/change log, then rewrite contradicted claims and find dependent analyses, parked readings, diagnosis and handoff. Do not rewrite a source quotation to fit the new interpretation. “No evidence found in the stated search” is narrower than “nobody has measured it.” Record an access blocker separately and stop when no appropriate authorized/affordable route remains, without labeling the blocked research gate passed.
