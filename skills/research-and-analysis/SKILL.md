# Company research before positioning

Build company understanding deep enough for a later positioning exercise to use without re-deriving the analysis. Use the sequence and investigative operations below. Carry over their questioning and corrections, never the conclusions of the analyses they came from. Separate observation, attributed claim, calculation, interpretation and hypothesis. A bounded unresolved verdict is useful; an inventory of features and citations is not a completed analysis.

## Frame the inquiry

Write or update the research brief: company/official domain and same-name exclusions, product/tier/version, markets, intended positioning use, freshness, inputs and constraints. A brief explains purpose; it does not determine the winning segment or story. Inspect existing work and triage it as reuse, verify or replace based on provenance, depth, scope and freshness. Ask only about unresolved choices that materially change the inquiry; missing internal evidence becomes a gap.

For a full inquiry use product → buyer → competitor → advantage → marketing → diagnosis → confidence audit → research handoff. Buyer and competitor may swap; advantage needs product/competitor understanding and marketing draws on all four. Focused follow-ups reuse relevant prior work and expose missing dependencies. Park marketing implications from earlier passes as short notes for the marketing analysis.

Keep evidence with its owning domain, company-wide facts in a profile, and shared query/gap registers. A full study produces five evidence/analysis pairs plus diagnosis, audit and handoff; use existing equivalents and create files as work begins. A focused follow-up may be one answer. Do not call a subset a full company analysis.

## Map sources before the domain passes

Read [evidence and gap protocol](references/evidence-and-gaps.md). Design a broad initial query set for identity, product, buyers, alternatives and commercial activity, with a serious contrary question for each. Map available documentation, releases, pricing, cases, partners, filings, measurement sources and independent accounts. Record what was read, not just found. This sweep yields evidence and questions, not strategy. Learn necessary vocabulary and specification literacy as needed; do not turn this into a course.

## Run a focused pass

1. Before each pass, read its section in [investigative procedures](references/company-passes.md) and the corresponding [worked calibration example](references/examples.md). Identify the operation and expected result to reproduce. Examples use fictional evidence; they are not facts or defaults for the researched company.
2. Read the brief, domain evidence, applicable prior conclusions and gap routes. Write a question-led outline. Collect and verify evidence, then perform the relevant operations: trace mechanisms, normalize quantities, reconstruct purchase decisions, compare equivalent alternatives or test a barrier to replication.
3. Write a consequential verdict with its reasoning, conditions, evidence and strongest relevant counter-reading. A technical capability, achieved customer outcome and durable advantage are different propositions. Show derived inputs/units and retain uncertainty.
4. End with **What this pass revealed we don't know**. Name each important gap's affected decision, attempted surfaces, next route, discriminating observation and status. If the first pass remains at feature level, seek deeper operational evidence; do not advance on citation volume.
5. Source deeper within scope/budget and rewrite affected claims. A failed premise reopens its originating pass and dependent analyses, parked notes, diagnosis and handoff. Preserve independent surviving support; keep history separately so the live record has one current answer.
6. Apply the [completion gates](references/completion-and-handoff.md) before advancing. Mark each pass complete, bounded with explicit open gates, or incomplete. A blocked check can permit bounded use; it does not count as passed. Stop at the budget or when remaining routes require unavailable access or offer no plausible decision-changing evidence. Never silently reduce explicit coverage.

## Check fit, synthesize and challenge

Apply frameworks only when their subject and unit of analysis fit; map criterion to evidence and answerable/partial/unknown. Missing internal information is not a failing score. Do not force an aggregate verdict from unsupported criteria.

Use [diagnosis, audit and handoff](references/completion-and-handoff.md) after the domain passes. Connect product, buyer conditions, alternatives, surviving advantages and marketing purchase support, then audit exact claims for existence, performance, prevalence and causality. Compare credible rival explanations with observable predictions and the smallest discriminating checks. Narrow the diagnosis and all dependents before handing it off. Keep useful hypotheses without presenting them as established company strategy.

Verify provenance separately from reasoning. A vendor report remains an attributed report; repetition is not independent validation. An unread page is not absence, public absence is not internal operating failure, and asset existence is not commercial performance. If public discussions are needed within the buyer pass, read [optional buyer collection](references/buyer-modes.md); its sampling controls are a later refinement, not a mandatory stage.

Deliver linked outputs, gate status, supported findings, consequential hypotheses and next checks. End at the research handoff. A lack of proven differentiation is valid. Choosing positioning, contacting people and campaigns require their own task scope.

## Lineage and limits

Primary lineage: the author's earlier company analyses, evidence, research decisions, marketing sourcing record and later confidence audit. The explicit pre-positioning audit/handoff is a deliberate correction to the historical sequence. A later refinement contributes version-aware reuse triage, downstream-usable verdicts and optional discussion-coverage controls. General-topic and standalone buyer-desk work stay excluded. The package uses newly authored procedures and synthetic calibration, without private source dependencies. This authored implementation is MIT licensed; no source corpus is distributed.

## Bundled positioning handoff

When used with dunford-positioning, pass the current evidence, diagnosis, audit
and handoff into its scope/current-stage review; none is an approved position.
Separate strategic fit, technical applicability, opportunity readiness and proof
readiness in candidate-fit inputs. Keep present differentiation separate from
durable barriers. For integration details use the companion's
`references/research-loop.md` when available; otherwise use this skill's native
focused-pass and handoff instructions.

A positioning request may initiate a bounded focused follow-up rather than a full
study. Preserve its originating decision, rival, budget and return point. Return
findings, evidence handles, changed claims, unresolved gaps and affected dependencies
to that decision. Repair owning records and affected handoff content; leave
positioning choices and approvals to the positioning review.

## Narrative follow-ups in the three-skill bundle

A narrative stage may request focused factual research. Retain its exact decision,
rival, effort limit and return point; update owning evidence and affected analyses.
Return to narrative without selecting a position or approving its next stage. If
findings invalidate positioning, identify the earliest affected premise for its
review rather than patching downstream copy. Companion skills remain optional.
