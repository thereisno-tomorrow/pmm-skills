# Method lineage and limits

This package is an original synthesis of reasoning methods applied to PMM. The references below identify intellectual lineage, not endorsements. No private vault extracts or third-party manuals are bundled. The newly authored instructions in this distribution are available under the [MIT License](../../../LICENSE). That license does not grant rights in linked third-party publications, names or marks.

- **Assumptions and information quality:** the US Government's [A Tradecraft Primer (2009)](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Tradecraft-Primer-apr09.pdf) informs targeted assumption and information checks. It is practitioner guidance. [Mandel, Karvetski and Dhami (2018)](https://sjdm.org/~baron/journal/18/18803/jdm18803.html) found no overall judgment-accuracy benefit for ACH in their constructed task. [Mandel and Tetlock (2018)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.02640/full) motivate evaluating correctives rather than assuming structured procedure improves judgment. No automatic ACH scoring is used here.
- **Arguments:** Toulmin's claim/grounds/warrant lineage, qualified by [Hitchcock's treatment of warrants](https://www.humanities.mcmaster.ca/~hitchckd/Toulminswarrants.pdf), informs faithful reconstruction without attributing invented premises. [Hernández (2023)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10503-023-09613-w) informs context-sensitive critical questions rather than compulsory scheme checklists.
- **Diagnosis:** [Klayman and Ha (1987)](https://pages.ucsd.edu/~mckenzie/KlaymanHaPsychReview1987.pdf) distinguish positive testing from biased confirmation. [Hernán and Robins, Causal Inference: What If](https://miguelhernan.org/whatifbook) informs the distinctions among association, intervention and causal identification. The package does not implement specialist estimation.
- **Decisions:** consequence comparison and objective/constraint distinctions draw on structured decision analysis. The UK Analysis Function's [introductory MCDA guide](https://analysisfunction.civilservice.gov.uk/policy-store/an-introductory-guide-to-mcda/) informs caution about scores, scales and weights. This package defaults to explicit consequences rather than a formal scoring engine.
- **Heuristics:** Kahneman/Tversky's judgment research, Simon's bounded rationality and Gigerenzer's ecological approach informed the development synthesis. They are competing and complementary research traditions, not one bias checklist. [Kahneman and Klein (2009)](https://gwern.net/doc/psychology/cognitive-bias/illusion-of-depth/2009-kahneman.pdf) informs checking regularities and useful feedback before treating intuition as expertise. Human studies do not establish that prompting a model to avoid a bias reproduces human intervention effects.

The 0.3.0 evidence-reconstruction procedure extends the claim/grounds/warrant and information-quality adaptations above. The update record and final-claim check are original workflow designs for preserving source status, dependencies and scope across revisions. They are not canonical CIA techniques. Recent research on model weaknesses motivates these reliability targets.

The 0.4.0 discriminating-diagnosis card turns the Klayman/Ha distinction into explicit mechanism predictions and comparisons with observed process traces. It uses the CIA primer as a source of structured comparison ideas without implementing ACH scores; critiques of ACH remain relevant cautions, not evidence against every individual operation. Positive tests can be useful when they distinguish live explanations; compulsory disconfirmation is not the rule. The intervention-comparison card adapts selected Hernán/Robins design ideas into population, assignment, exposure, denominator and follow-up checks. It adds no specialist estimator. Both cards are original PMM workflow adaptations.

The domain adaptations and presentation rules are this package's own design. Naming a concept does not by itself make an answer correct.
