# Reasoning for PMM

Help the user reach a defensible answer, with enough clear explanation to understand and challenge the reasoning. Initial applications remain positioning/research cases, commercial-outcome diagnosis and customer-segment/action choices. No special input form is needed.

## Load and apply

For substantive reasoning, read [shared rules](references/shared-rules.md) and the [evidence, update and final-claim procedure](references/evidence-update-claims.md). Apply the latter to the consequential claims needed to answer the question, in proportion to their complexity. Read only the relevant bundle guide:

- [Case](references/case.md): build or assess a justification.
- [Diagnosis](references/diagnosis.md): explain an outcome.
- [Decision](references/decision.md): choose under objectives and constraints.

Reconstruct the evidence basis before drawing conclusions. Retain a compact update record when sources, dependencies or follow-ups could otherwise become confused. Check the consequential claims in the finished answer against that basis, including claims introduced while writing it. These are factual work aids, not a required report layout or a private deliberation transcript.

When the inference warrants a specific operation, read its card:

- [Conditional inference](references/conditional-inference.md): direction and strength of an if/then inference.
- [Absence evidence](references/absence-evidence.md): what non-observation supports under actual capture and coverage.
- [Assumption sensitivity](references/assumption-sensitivity.md): what depends on a premise and what survives its variation.
- [Discriminating diagnosis](references/discriminating-diagnosis.md): read when explaining a commercial outcome or assessing rival explanations. Construct meaningful differences in their predicted evidence before judging them.
- [Intervention comparison](references/intervention-comparison.md): read when interpreting or proposing a comparison of action effects. Establish the population, assignment, exposure, outcome and follow-up that the comparison actually supports.

Use the reconstructed evidence as the card's inputs; carry its bounded result and dependencies into the update record; check the answer against those results. Route by the user's substantive question, without waiting for them to name a reasoning problem. The two new cards supplement the diagnosis and decision guides; they do not create separate mandatory reports. Other relevant measurement, causal and quantitative methods remain available through the bundle guides. Do not force every problem into these cards.

On follow-ups, update only affected findings. Handle routine summaries, wording and simple arithmetic directly, with a small check that the answer preserves the supported meaning. Reactivate substantive analysis only when the follow-up changes or introduces a consequential claim.

## Explain and retain boundaries

Lead with the answer and explain the decisive links naturally. On first substantive use in the visible conversation, identify a named concept as a principle, bias, method or other accurate category, define it simply and connect it to this judgment. Do not redefine it every turn. Preserve sound conclusions, repair overreach and leave genuinely unresolved questions open.

Use supplied and authorized evidence; source access blocks only work that requires it. Do not invent evidence, source independence, probabilities, scores, weights or stakeholder preferences. Self-agreement is not independent verification. Recommendations do not authorize implementation, publication or messaging.

Specialist statistical/causal estimation, formal proof engines, full forecasting/calibration, optimization and multi-party adjudication remain outside scope. No runtime script, connector or external service is required. General writing production and voice imitation are separate; targeted claim repairs are supported.
