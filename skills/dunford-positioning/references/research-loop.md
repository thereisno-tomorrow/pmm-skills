# Research and positioning handoff loop

Read this when moving from company research into positioning or when a positioning
decision exposes a consequential evidence gap. Both skills remain usable alone.
The bundle adds coordination between them.

## Enter positioning

Use the research brief, current diagnosis, confidence audit, handoff and existing
evidence/gap ledgers. Read underlying analyses only as needed to assess a material
claim. Reuse adequate work; do not repeat the full research sequence by default.
If useful evidence already exists, start positioning directly and investigate gaps
as needed. A full company study is optional, not an entrance requirement.

Begin at Dunford Stage 0 for a new exercise, or the current reviewed stage when
resuming. Research completion is neither positioning approval nor customer
validation. Keep research interpretations as candidates. In the relevant packet,
record which are retained, conditional, branched, rejected or reopened, and why.

Carry forward product/version and responsibility boundaries; observed versus
plausible alternatives; relative capabilities; candidate value mechanisms;
customer circumstances and language; proof and unsafe claims; open questions;
and source handles, confidence reasons and use limits. Confidence and logical
status are separate: a plausible inference is not an established observation.

Separate strategic fit (who values the consequence most), technical applicability,
opportunity readiness (trigger, budget and approval), and proof readiness. A lack
of present budget does not disqualify strategic fit. Keep present differentiation
separate from replication barriers: no demonstrated moat does not mean no useful
relative value. Current marketing is evidence of inherited framing and purchase
support, not authority for the new position.

## Request a focused research round

At any positioning stage, first check whether existing evidence answers the
question. Research is warranted when a missing or contested premise could change
alternatives, differentiation, value, fit, category or permitted use. Do not
restart research merely to fill a heading or seek reassurance.

Record a compact request in the active packet or shared gap ledger:

- Originating stage/branch and exact decision at risk.
- Current proposition, evidence and strongest rival explanation.
- Discriminating question and observation that could favor either reading.
- Relevant research pass(es), plausible sources, access and freshness needs.
- Bounded effort/time/source budget and stopping condition, using existing task
  constraints where adequate; intended return stage and affected dependencies.

Use the companion research-and-analysis skill for only those passes and necessary
dependencies. Keep the current positioning review pending. Use already-authorized
research within scope; propose an expansion before exceeding the assignment.
Unavailable private evidence becomes a request or unresolved gap. This loop grants
no outreach, interview, private-data upload or publication authority.

## Return to the decision

Research returns the exact question, sources actually read, supported findings,
changed/unchanged claims, counterevidence, remaining unknowns, gate status and
implications for the pending decision. Record new evidence in the existing owning
ledger; repair the originating analysis, diagnosis/audit/handoff where affected.
Do not create a second competing source record or repeat unaffected passes.

Stop at the budget, when a discriminating answer is obtained, or when no plausible
authorized route remains. A failed fetch or exhausted search is not evidence that
the capability, demand or customer behavior is absent. Do not cycle indefinitely:
an additional round needs a new plausible route and a decision worth its cost.

Return to the same positioning decision and present its revised review packet.
If the finding invalidates an accepted upstream premise, reopen its originating
stage first and mark dependent approvals/artifacts stale; preserve independent
surviving facts and branches. Do not silently retain an invalid approved chain.

If the gap remains, offer a bounded hypothesis/branch, a narrower claim, a request
for unavailable evidence or a pause, with consequences. Research completion
cannot approve the stage. Obtain the normal human review decision before moving
to the next positioning unit. Only an explicit user change to the review contract
can alter that requirement.

## Worked illustrations

**Normal loop:** Stage 4 considers an integration advantage. A rival release may
have added parity. Request a current, configuration-matched competitor/product
check within the agreed budget. If parity is documented, update the comparison
and return a revised differentiation packet for review; do not proceed directly
to value or rerun the full company study.

**Counterexample:** Stage 6 needs actual reasons customers selected the product,
but only vendor cases are public and no private access is available. Record the
unresolved question and bounded fit hypotheses. More desk research cannot become
win/loss evidence. Present the limitation and requested decision, without an
endless research loop or a claim that public silence means low customer value.

**Upstream reversal:** At Stage 7 a focused check disproves a prerequisite used
in the approved Stage 3 comparison. Reopen Stage 3 and flag dependent Stages 4–7
for revision. The return point is the earliest invalid decision, not a patched
category story built on the old premise.

## Narrative return path

Narrative work may reveal a missing product fact or a strategic conflict. Request a
bounded research round for the former, preserving its source decision and return
point. Reopen positioning for the latter, marking dependent narrative claims stale.
After actual review, send the changed position/claim permissions back to the same
narrative stage; do not silently continue from superseded approvals or restart
unaffected work. Narrative approval cannot clear positioning's unresolved evidence
or publish an artifact. If no credible external change exists, let the user choose
a situation-led reframe or narrower explanation; do not manufacture a trend.
